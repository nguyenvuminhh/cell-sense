# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** December 2025

## Data We Collect

- **User queries:** Your text prompts sent to the AI
- **Selected data ranges:** Spreadsheet data you share for context
- **Chat history:** Stored to provide conversation context
- **Account info:** Basic user identification

## How We Use Data

- Process your requests and generate formula suggestions
- Improve service quality
- Maintain chat history for context

## Third-Party Services

Your queries may be sent to LLM providers (Google, OpenAI, Anthropic) for processing. Their privacy policies apply to data they receive.

## Data Storage

Data is stored on Google Cloud Platform with standard security measures.

## Data Retention

Chat history is retained until you delete it or your account.

## Your Rights

- Access your data
- Delete your chat history
- Delete your account

## Contact

For privacy concerns, contact the project maintainer.
